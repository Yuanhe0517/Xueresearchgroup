I am an undergraduate student majoring in Physics in the School of Science at Westlake University. My research interests include soft matter mechanics, biomechanics, and the mechanical behavior of cells and tissues. My current work involves numerical simulations using two-dimensional vertex models to investigate the deformation and collective mechanical responses of cell sheets. Through this work, I aim to connect physical principles and computational modeling with questions in biological systems.
